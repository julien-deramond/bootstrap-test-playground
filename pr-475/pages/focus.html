Focus rings

Every focusable component on bg-body, bg-1 to bg-3 and every theme's solid background, for every theme color. The accessibility scan focuses each one and measures its ring, and the visual suite captures them focused.

A ring passes when the pixels it changes contrast at least 3:1 with what was there before focus, over at least the area of a 2px perimeter of the component (WCAG 2.4.13). See #105, and #7 for chips.
