Issue pg-255

Steps to reproduce

  1. Run axe-core on this page with the WCAG 2.2 AA tags: scrollable-region-focusable flags .carousel-inner and target-size flags each inactive indicator.
  2. Inspect .carousel-inner: it scrolls (overflow-x: auto), has no tabindex and holds no focusable element, only svg role="img" placeholders.
  3. Measure the indicator buttons: 12×12px, 4px apart.

Expected

A keyboard user can reach the scroll viewport (.carousel-inner has a tabindex other than -1, or holds a focusable element), and every indicator is at least 24×24px or leaves 24px of room around its dot (WCAG 2.5.8).

Actual

The viewport has no tabindex and no focusable content, and the indicators are 12px dots 4px apart, so their 24px circles overlap.
