Issue pg-185

Steps to reproduce

  1. Click the input below to open the datepicker. This page uses the large-type config, where the type scale is one step larger than the default.
  2. Click Measure the calendar's text. It opens the calendar, reads the computed font-size of the month and year buttons, a weekday and a day, sets --datepicker-font-size: 2rem on the calendar and reads them again.

Expected

The calendar's text follows --datepicker-font-size, so a larger value or a larger type scale makes the month and year buttons, the weekdays and the days larger.

Actual

Only the calendar container changes. The month and year buttons stay at 16px, and the weekdays and days at 12px, because each sets its own font-size in rem.
