Issue pg-270

Steps to reproduce

  1. Put a focusable component, here a button, on bg-body, bg-1, bg-2 and bg-3, in light and dark mode.
  2. Tab to it and measure its focus ring (--bs-primary-focus-ring) against the surface behind it.
  3. The full matrix, every component and theme, is in /pages/focus.html.

Expected

The ring contrasts at least 3:1 with what's behind it (WCAG 1.4.11 and 2.4.13), on every surface and in both modes.

Actual

The ring is the theme color mixed 50% (light) or 75% (dark) with --bg-body, whatever the surface it sits on. It fails 3:1 in light mode everywhere and in dark mode on bg-2 and bg-3.
