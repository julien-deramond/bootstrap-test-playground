Issue pg-133: range fill and bubble in RTL

Steps to reproduce

  1. Render a .form-range with data-bs-bubble inside dir="rtl", with a value of 20 out of 100.
  2. Compare where the filled part of the track ends and where the bubble sits with the thumb, which sits near the right edge.

Expected

The fill runs from the right edge, the inline start, up to the thumb, and the bubble is centered on the thumb.

Actual

The fill is painted from the left edge, so it covers the part of the track the thumb has not reached, and the bubble is placed from the left, so it sits on the far side of the track.
