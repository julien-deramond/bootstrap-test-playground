Issue pg-139: datepicker arrows and range corners in RTL

Steps to reproduce

  1. Render an inline datepicker in dir="rtl", in range mode, with a range already selected (below).
  2. Look at the arrows in the header, then at the two ends of the selected range.

Expected

The previous arrow, on the right, points right and the next arrow, on the left, points left. The first day of the range is rounded on its right side and the last day on its left side.

Actual

Both arrows are rotated as in LTR, so they point inward, and the first and last days keep the LTR corners: the first is rounded on its left side, the last on its right side.
