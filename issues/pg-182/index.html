Issue pg-182

Steps to reproduce

  1. Compile Bootstrap with $theme-colors where info and secondary are null (the brand config does it, see main.scss).
  2. Look at the strength meter below: its password is already at the "good" level, so three of four segments are filled.
  3. Look at the two disabled labels below: the radio's and the checkbox's.

Expected

The "good" segments are filled with a color, and the disabled radio's label is dimmed like the disabled checkbox's. Removing a theme should not break a component that reads it.

Actual

The "good" segments stay empty, because --strength-good-color: var(--info-bg) points to a token that is no longer defined. The disabled radio's label keeps the body color, because var(--secondary-fg) is undefined too, while the disabled checkbox's label is dimmed with var(--fg-3). The weak, fair and strong levels have the same problem when danger, warning or success is removed.
