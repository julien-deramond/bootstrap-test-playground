Responsive placement ignores custom $breakpoints

Steps to reproduce

  1. Open this page at 1400px wide. This page uses the breakpoints-custom config: md is renamed tablet (768px), 2xl is removed and 3xl is 1920px.
  2. Show each tooltip, popover and menu. The placement of every one starts at top (bottom for menus) and has a single responsive prefix: tablet:, md: or 3xl:.
  3. Read the placement Bootstrap applies (data-bs-placement on the tooltip, the popover and the menu), then widen the window to 1920px and read it again.

Expected

The prefixes are the breakpoints of the compiled CSS. At 1400px the tablet: case is placed to the right, while md: (no longer a breakpoint) and 3xl: stay on top. At 1920px the 3xl: case is placed to the right too.

Actual

Only sm, md, lg, xl and 2xl at their default widths are recognized. md: is placed to the right from 768px, while tablet: and 3xl: are ignored.
