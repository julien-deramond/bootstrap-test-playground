Issue pg-156

Steps to reproduce

  1. Click the combobox toggle. The popup opens and focus stays on the toggle.
  2. Press Escape.

Expected

The popup closes, aria-expanded becomes false, focus stays on the toggle and hide.bs.combobox and hidden.bs.combobox fire.

Actual

Nothing happens: the popup stays open, aria-expanded stays true and no hide event fires. Escape only works once focus is on an option or in the search input.
