Select caret stays black on dark pages unless data-bs-theme="dark" is set

Steps to reproduce

  1. Look at the caret of each select below. The first block follows the OS: put the OS (or the toolbar's color mode, on Auto) in dark mode. The other blocks are dark whatever the OS says.
  2. Compare with the data-bs-theme="dark" block, the reference. Its caret is white at 50%; the others stay black at 50%, so nearly invisible on their dark control.
  3. In the last block, a data-bs-theme="light" subtree inside a dark one, the inverse happens: the caret is white on a light control.

Expected

The caret follows the color scheme its control is rendered in: light on a dark control (#ffffff80), dark on a light one (#00000080), like every light-dark() color around it.

Actual

The dark caret (--control-select-bg-dark) is applied under [data-bs-theme="dark"] only, as a descendant selector. A dark color-scheme from the OS, from .color-scheme-dark or from a page default keeps the black caret, and a light subtree of a dark page gets the white one.

Reproduction

1. Follows the OS

No attribute, no class: color-scheme: light dark on :root. Dark when the OS is.

.form-control-caret

2. .color-scheme-dark

A wrapper with the utility, with any OS setting.

.form-control-caret

3. Dark by default

A wrapper whose color-scheme is dark, as the dark-first config sets it for the page.

.form-control-caret

Reference: data-bs-theme="dark"

The one case that works today.

.form-control-caret

4. Light subtree of a dark page

A data-bs-theme="light" block inside a data-bs-theme="dark" one.

.form-control-caret
