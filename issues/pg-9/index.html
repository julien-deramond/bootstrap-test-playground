Issue pg-9: nav and range tokens

Steps to reproduce

  1. Put a .nav in bold text, as below.
  2. Render a range with the defaults, where $enable-shadows is true.
  3. Inspect the nav link's font-weight, and look for --range-track-box-shadow on the range.

Expected

Every token a rule reads without a fallback is declared in a token map: the nav link has a weight of its own, and the range track gets its shadow. Both show up in the docs' token tables.

Actual

.nav-link reads var(--nav-link-font-weight) and the range track var(--range-track-box-shadow), and no map declares them. The link inherits the bold weight, and the track has no shadow.
