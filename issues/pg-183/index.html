Issue pg-183: default palette contrast

Steps to reproduce

  1. Compile Bootstrap with its default config, as this reproduction's main.scss does.
  2. For each theme color, look at the solid button below (its contrast text on its bg fill) and at the fg text on the body background, in light and dark mode.
  3. Measure the WCAG 2 ratio of each pairing. Colors are resolved in the browser and gamut-mapped to sRGB first.

Expected

Every solid fill and every fg text on the body background reaches 4.5:1, the WCAG AA minimum for normal text.

Actual

White on success (2.82), primary (3.56) and danger (4.42) fills, and in light mode the success (4.34), warning (3.96) and info (3.71) text, are under 4.5:1.
