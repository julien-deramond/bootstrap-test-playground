Issue pg-136: avatar stack overlap in RTL

Steps to reproduce

  1. Render an .avatar-stack of four avatars once in dir="ltr" and once in dir="rtl" (below), in a container that is exactly as wide as the stack.
  2. Compare the gap between each pair of neighbors, and the stack's edges with the container's. This is the pattern of the avatar stack examples.

Expected

The RTL stack is the mirror image of the LTR one: every avatar overlaps its neighbor by the same amount (--bs-avatar-size * --bs-avatar-stack-spacing) and the first and last avatars sit flush with the stack's edges.

Actual

In RTL the negative margin-left lands on the other side: the pair at one end does not overlap, and the first avatar's margin pulls it out of the container at the start edge.
