Issue #31088: Dismiss-on-next-click popovers close on a click inside

Steps to reproduce

  1. Start from the Popover: Dismiss on next click docs example, copied below.
  2. Click the button to open the popover.
  3. Select some of the popover's text, or click inside it.
  4. Then click outside the popover.
  5. Fixed by twbs/bootstrap#42834 (0b62385), which the installed Bootstrap includes: the page shows the fix.

Expected

A click or a selection inside the popover keeps it open. The next click outside closes it.

Actual

Fixed upstream: it stays open. Before the fix, pressing inside the popover moved the focus out of the trigger, and the focus trigger hid the popover.

Measured here: open the popover, then click inside it

Reproduction

Dismissible popover
