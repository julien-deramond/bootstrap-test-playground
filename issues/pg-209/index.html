Issue pg-209

Steps to reproduce

  1. This page uses the high-contrast config, which sets --control-disabled-opacity: 1 (and --btn-disabled-opacity: 1).
  2. Look at the disabled switches, the chip and the combobox toggle below, then at the disabled check and the chip inside a disabled chip input.
  3. Inspect the computed opacity of each.

Expected

Every disabled control follows --control-disabled-opacity, directly or through a component token that defaults to it, so all of them are fully opaque here.

Actual

The disabled checked switch, the chip and the combobox toggle stay at .65, and the indicator of the unchecked switch at .4. The check, the chip input and the button read their tokens and are opaque.
