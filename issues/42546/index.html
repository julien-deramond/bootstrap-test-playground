Issue #42546: scrollbar-gutter: stable leaves an empty gutter

Steps to reproduce

  1. Use a browser with classic scrollbars: Chromium on Windows as in the issue, on Linux, or on macOS with “Show scroll bars: Always”. Overlay scrollbars take no room, so there's nothing to see.
  2. Open this page without its chrome: it's too short to scroll. As in the issue, tokens.css draws a red border around html and body.
  3. Look at the inline end of the page, then switch to scrollbar-gutter: auto.
  4. Bootstrap sets scrollbar-gutter: stable on :root (scss/_root.scss) so that the page doesn't shift when a dialog or drawer hides its scrollbar.

Expected

A page that doesn't scroll uses the whole width of the window, as with Bootstrap 5.

Actual

An empty strip as wide as a scrollbar stays on the inline end of every page, between the red border and the window's edge, and full-width backgrounds stop short of it.
