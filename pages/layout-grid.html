Layout: grid

The flexbox grid: .row and its columns, with the responsive prefixes (md:col-6), the gutters, offsets and order. Resize the window, or use the toolbar's Show breakpoint, to see each example change at its breakpoint. The prefixes are the config's: with breakpoints-custom, md: is gone and the examples that use it stay stacked. See also CSS grid and containers.
